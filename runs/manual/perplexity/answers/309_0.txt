For a 20-person B2B SaaS startup with no SDR team, I'd choose **Apollo + HubSpot** for the best balance of AI prospecting, sequencing, CRM, and cost. If UK/EU data compliance is your highest priority, choose **Cognism or Dealfront for data**, then use a controlled outreach tool such as **Lemlist** rather than relying on an autonomous AI SDR.

## Best platforms

| Platform | Best for | Strengths | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Apollo.io** | Best all-in-one startup option | Prospect database, AI research, enrichment, sequences, dialer, CRM integrations, affordable entry point | US-based provider; you must review international transfers, source provenance, and lawful-basis documentation |
| **Cognism** | Highest-confidence UK/EU prospect data | Strong UK and European coverage, phone verification, TPS/CTPS and other do-not-call screening, privacy and DSAR processes | Usually quote-based and more expensive; still requires a separate CRM and engagement workflow |
| **Dealfront** | Europe-first and EU-hosted prospecting | European company data, website visitor identification, intent signals, public-register sourcing, EU-focused privacy posture | Less suitable if you need a fully built-in multichannel outbound engine |
| **Lemlist** | Compliant, personalized outbound execution | Email sequences, AI personalization, multichannel workflows, DPA, EEA data storage according to its documentation | It is primarily an engagement layer, so data quality depends on your data provider |
| **Clay** | Advanced enrichment and custom AI workflows | Connects multiple data providers, supports AI research and personalization, highly flexible | Compliance depends on every connected data source and subprocessor; not ideal as the first tool for a team with no SDR process |
| **Outreach or Salesloft** | Mature enterprise sales operations | Strong governance, sequencing, analytics, coaching, and CRM controls | Generally excessive cost and complexity for a 20-person startup without SDRs |

Apollo documents UK/EU notices, opt-out controls, DPA terms, data-subject workflows, and the ability to exclude EU contacts from prospecting and emailing. That makes it a practical startup choice, but not an automatic legal solution. [knowledge.apollo](https://knowledge.apollo.io/hc/en-us/articles/4409141087757-General-Data-Protection-Regulation-GDPR-Overview)

Cognism states that it screens telephone data against UK TPS/CTPS and other national do-not-call lists, performs privacy assessments, supports DSARs, and maintains GDPR-related documentation. Dealfront states that it uses a legitimate-interest basis for business contact data and provides a DPA, while positioning itself around European data sources. [help.cognism](https://help.cognism.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4406236439826-What-has-Cognism-done-internally-to-comply-with-GDPR-and-data-privacy-regulations)

## My recommended stack

### Option 1: Best overall

**Apollo + HubSpot CRM + Google Workspace/Microsoft 365**

Use Apollo to identify accounts, find contacts, generate research briefs, and run small, carefully targeted sequences. Keep HubSpot as the system of record for lifecycle stages, suppression lists, replies, opportunities, and unsubscribe status.

This is the simplest option when nobody has yet built an SDR process. Start with a small number of high-fit accounts rather than immediately sending thousands of emails.

### Option 2: Best for UK/EU compliance

**Dealfront or Cognism + HubSpot + Lemlist**

Use Dealfront when EU hosting, European company data, and source traceability matter most. Use Cognism when verified UK/EU phone data and country-specific telephone screening are especially important. Send campaigns through Lemlist, whose documentation describes a GDPR DPA and EEA storage. [lemlist](https://www.lemlist.com/de/legal/dpa)

This stack costs more and involves more integrations, but it separates **data quality** from **outreach execution**, making vendor and compliance reviews clearer.

### Option 3: Best for sophisticated personalization

**Cognism or Apollo + Clay + HubSpot + Lemlist**

Use Clay only after your ICP, messaging, suppression rules, and review process are working. It is powerful for combining firmographic, technographic, hiring, funding, and website signals, but every connected provider must be assessed individually; Clay's DPA does not make all external data sources compliant by itself. [explore.openli](https://explore.openli.com/privacy/clay-3/data-processing-agreements)

## GDPR and UK outreach requirements

"GDPR-compliant platform" does not mean every campaign sent through it is lawful. Your company remains responsible for the purpose, lawful basis, targeting, message, retention, and handling of objections.

For UK campaigns, the ICO says that marketing to corporate subscribers by email generally does not require PECR consent, but you still need a valid UK GDPR lawful basis—often legitimate interests—and must distinguish corporate subscribers from sole traders and certain partnerships. The ICO also says legitimate interests is not automatic: you must complete the three-part test and comply with other marketing laws. [ico.org](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/)

For EU campaigns, document a legitimate-interest assessment, explain who you are and why you are contacting the person, provide an immediate objection/unsubscribe method, minimize stored data, and maintain a suppression list. The EDPB describes legitimate interest as requiring a legitimate purpose, necessity, and a balancing test against the individual's rights. [edpb.europa](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/edpb-adopts-opinion-on-processors-guidelines-on-legitimate-interest-statement-on-draft_en)

## Minimum operating controls

Before sending, configure:

- Country and subscriber-type rules, especially for sole traders and individual professionals.
- A documented legitimate-interest assessment for each campaign or market.
- Vendor DPA, subprocessor list, international-transfer mechanism, retention terms, and deletion process.
- Source and collection date for each contact.
- Privacy notice link or first-touch transparency wording.
- One-click unsubscribe and a central, permanent suppression list.
- TPS/CTPS screening for UK phone outreach and equivalent national registers where applicable.
- Human approval for AI-generated claims, personalization, and contact selection.
- Conservative limits: for example, start with 20–40 highly relevant new contacts per sender per day and monitor complaints, bounces, and positive replies.

## Final choice

I would shortlist **Apollo**, **Cognism**, and **Dealfront** for evaluation:

1. **Apollo** if budget, speed, and an all-in-one workflow matter most.
2. **Cognism** if UK/EU contact quality and compliance evidence justify a premium price.
3. **Dealfront** if European data residency, public-register sourcing, and intent signals are central.

For your situation, the most sensible pilot is **Apollo + HubSpot for 30 days**, with a compliance review before launch. If Apollo's UK/EU data coverage or legal documentation is not sufficient, move to **Cognism or Dealfront for the data layer** and keep **Lemlist** for controlled execution.
