## Core credibility issues

- **Fabricated customer claims and testimonials:** A March 2025 TechCrunch investigation reported that 11x listed companies (e.g., ZoomInfo, Airtable) as customers without authorization and published customer testimonials that were denied by the quoted individuals or companies. [techcrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/24/a16z-and-benchmark-backed-11x-has-been-claiming-customers-it-doesnt-have/)
- **Inflated growth metrics (ARR):** Former employees and sources alleged 11x counted full-year contract value toward annual recurring revenue (ARR) even when customers exercised early "break clauses" and left after ~3 months, making growth look stronger than committed revenue. [techcrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/24/a16z-and-benchmark-backed-11x-has-been-claiming-customers-it-doesnt-have/)
- **Very high early churn:** Multiple sources (former/current employees, industry observers) report ~70–80% of customers churn within the first 3 months; one 2026 review cites ~75% 3-month churn. [techcrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/24/a16z-and-benchmark-backed-11x-has-been-claiming-customers-it-doesnt-have/)
- **Product performance complaints:** Reports include AI "hallucinations," features not working as expected, inconsistent results, and messaging that can feel generic or robotic—leading to more manual work than promised. [techcrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/24/a16z-and-benchmark-backed-11x-has-been-claiming-customers-it-doesnt-have/)
- **Contract rigidity and pricing opacity:** Buyers note a high minimum annual commitment (often cited around $60K), no pilots, opaque pricing until sales conversations, and anxiety around credits/usage visibility. [thecroreport](https://thecroreport.com/tools/11x/)
