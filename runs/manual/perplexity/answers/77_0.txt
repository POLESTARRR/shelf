## Recommended Stack for Your Situation

Given you're a **20-person startup with no SDR team**, a pragmatic starting stack would be:

- **Apollo.io (free or starter plan)** – for database + sequencing + basic AI. [intempt](https://intempt.com/blog/top-ai-sales-prospecting-tools)
- **Instantly** – if you plan to run high-volume cold email from multiple domains. [marketbetter](https://www.marketbetter.ai/blog/best-ai-sdr-tools/)
- **Origami (free tier)** – to quickly generate verified prospect lists from natural-language ICP descriptions. [origami](https://origami.chat/blog/best-ai-sales-prospecting-tools-sdrs-small-businesses-2026)

This combo gives you:
- Lead discovery (Origami + Apollo)
- Multi-channel sequencing (Apollo + Instantly)
- AI personalization and writing assistance (built into all three)
- Low upfront cost and minimal ops burden [marketbetter](https://www.marketbetter.ai/blog/best-ai-sdr-tools/)

Once you're consistently booking meetings, you can evaluate upgrading to an autonomous agent like **11x** or **Artisan**, or layer in a conversation intelligence tool (e.g., Gong, Avoma) for call coaching. [vezadigital](https://www.vezadigital.com/post/best-ai-sales-tools)
